The AI half of the factory — a reusable foundation for building LLM-powered products without re-stitching the same primitives every time.

## What it gives you

- **RAG** — pluggable retrievers, chunking strategies, hybrid search hooks.
- **Agent workflows** — LangGraph-based, with state checkpointing and resumable runs.
- **Eval harness** — pre/post-LLM scoring; flagged retrieval failures don&apos;t get blamed on the model anymore.
- **Tool calling** — typed tool registry, schema validation, retries with backoff.
- **Observability** — every LLM call is logged with prompt, output, latency, cost — so debugging isn&apos;t archaeology.

## Why this layer matters

In every team I&apos;ve seen build with LLMs, the model is rarely the bottleneck — retrieval, tool wiring, eval discipline, and observability are. AI Core makes those the default, not an afterthought.
