A reusable Java/Spring backend foundation I can drop into any SaaS or AI product I build next — so I stop re-solving auth, multi-tenancy, persistence, and ops every time.

## Why this exists

After 5+ years shipping enterprise backends, I kept re-implementing the same primitives. For AI products specifically, the unsexy backend (eval logging, agent state, rate limits, billing, audit) is where most teams stall — so I&apos;m carving it out once.

## What it gives you

- Auth + RBAC + audit logging out of the box.
- Multi-tenant ready (tenant boundary baked into queries).
- PostgreSQL with sensible migration patterns.
- Container-first; one-line local boot via Docker Compose.
- Sane Spring conventions — package-by-feature, no anemic-domain anti-patterns.

## Status

- Core skeleton in place. Iterating against the AI Core and the Service & Product Factory in parallel.
- No version cut yet — internal use to validate the abstractions before locking them.
