I&apos;ve been writing in private docs for years and not shipping any of it.
This blog is the un-shipping.

What goes here:

- Concrete notes from building with LLMs at work (RAG, evals, tool use, agent loops). I&apos;ll use real numbers when I have them.
- Project recaps in a fixed format: what I did, what it looked like before, what changed, what happened, what it still doesn&apos;t do.
- Occasionally, a concept explained in four registers — the raw version I tell myself, the LinkedIn version that gets engagement, the interview-grade version, and the structural pattern underneath.

What does not go here: "lessons learned" sections, life analogies, mind-blowing revelations, "what do you think?" prompts at the end of posts.

If you read something here that sounds like a guru talking, tell me. I&apos;ll fix it.
