Knowledge cards are short, atomic, ideally one-concept-per-file. Optimized for RAG retrieval rather than narrative reading.

Convention:

- Lead with the definition.
- One H2 per facet (when, why, how, edge cases).
- Cite `sources` in frontmatter — not inline link soup.
- Mark `confidence` so retrievers can prefer facts over opinions.

This is `unlisted` by default — it&apos;s in the RAG manifest and reachable by direct URL, but doesn&apos;t show up in `/`, `/posts`, sitemap, or RSS.
